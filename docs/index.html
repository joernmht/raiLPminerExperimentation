Why only 3 papers in the demo? The public demo ships only papers published under a CC-BY licence (verified via OpenAlex), and is deliberately focused on a small sample of simple, single-objective MILP formulations. The full 238-paper corpus was mined under Elsevier text-and-data-mining terms and is reproduced from the pipeline, not redistributed.
Part of the LP2Graph paper series — LP Mining with LP2Graph: A Use Case for Railway Rescheduling (TU Dresden, Chair of Railway Operations). Pipeline & code: joernmht/raiLPminerExperimentation · library: lp2graph · sister demo: lp2graph — typed graphs & configurator.
